HOLI: FELT

Worked on production and lighting for "Holi: Felt," a cultural photoshoot for FASTatUCLA.

Holi, the South Asian “festival of colors,” celebrates spring, renewal, and connection. This editorial explores the celebration as an emotional and physical language. Gulal powder becomes intentional pigment on skin. Color marks intimacy, trust, and most importantly, the journey of finding play. Holi has always been about breaking barriers: between people, between seasons, between who we were and who we’re becoming. We honor that through progression; the way color and closeness build, the warmth of being seen, the moments that give way to laughter and freedom. Here, Holi becomes a lens for discovery. A way to explore transformation through color, touch, and presence.
